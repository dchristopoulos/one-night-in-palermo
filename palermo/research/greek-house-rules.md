# Greek house rules and play culture for Palermo (Πέφτει η νύχτα στο Παλέρμο)

Research notes, 2026-11-14. Adds to `palermo-rules.md` (PR) and `roles-catalog.md` (RC); those notes are not repeated here except where a new source contradicts or extends them. Only sources opened in this run are cited as read. Search-snippet-only facts are marked *snippet*.

## (a) Summary

1. Greek web search is thin: most results are about the Palermo city, escape games, or the 2013 Kaissa board game. No Reddit, insomnia.gr or YouTube thread on the rules was found.
2. New Greek sources opened this run: pillowfights.gr (2024), kidsproject.gr, the Qbrain blog index (palermopaixnidi), the all4fun reader comments, two Palermo app store listings, and the palermomafia.com FAQ.
3. The Qbrain card list adds rule details not in PR or RC: the Bulletproof card works only against night kills, Resurrection triggers right after any death, and the Dream card lets one yes/no question go to the narrator.
4. Palermo Nights' FAQ (on palermomafia.com after a 301) says the first eliminated player narrates the night, ties go to discussion, voted-out cards are revealed, and a claimed role may be believed or not as part of the game.
5. Narrator phrases are consistent across the new sources: «Πέφτει η νύχτα στο Παλέρμο», «Μια νέα μέρα ξημερώνει στο Παλέρμο», plus a «κλείνουμε όλοι τα μάτια μας» variant. These match PR section 3.
6. Narrator humour is documented: one all4fun narrator opened with a personal, joking story and the first night was loud with laughter and quips.
7. Fun, per the sources: shouting, all-night sessions, teasing over body language, bluffing like poker, and the group as the real winner. Pillowfights.gr is the only source with specific accusation lines.
8. The Kaissa board game (2013) is a different design (hidden role abilities, action cards, 7–20 players). Its designer name differs between two search snippets, so it is marked unverified.
9. Optional evil helper: pillowfights.gr lists an Ιερόδουλη (courtesan) that supports the killers and knows them, adding one more source to PR row "evil informed helper".
10. Nothing here overturns the PR or RC consensus. The new material is mostly flavour, card details and narrator style.

## 2. House rules on voting, reveals, dead players, narrator

**Narrator and first dead player.** The first player out runs the night in the Palermo Nights FAQ (https://palermomafia.com/faqs/, reached by a 301 from https://palermonights.gr/faqs/). Also in the all4fun article, where the narrator is a human who cues every phase. Consistent with PR section 2.1.

**Voting and ties.** Active players vote for whoever they find suspicious; the most votes leaves and the card is shown; in a tie, discuss again, following the app's instructions (https://palermomafia.com/faqs/). Consistent with PR section 2.3.

**Reveal rules.** A player eliminated at night may not show their card unless someone with Τυμβωρυχία asks (Qbrain: http://palermopaixnidi.blogspot.com/). Consistent with PR and RC.

**Dead players.** The dead can take part in the Crystal Ball vote and the Τυμβωρυχία effect reveals a dead player's identity (Qbrain). The kidsproject article says eliminated players stop taking part, but the first ejected player relays the night choice to the group (https://kidsproject.gr/articles/470/impostor-sto-palermo-hdh-eteron). This relay is a new detail not in PR. Last words are not covered in any Greek source opened here.

**Police and win condition.** The kidsproject article says the killers win by eliminating the police officer, and that day and night keep alternating while at least one killer remains. This is the classic rule (PR section 2.4).

**Police signal.** kidsproject: the police officer «helps indirectly without revealing their identity» and the all4fun article has the first ejected player give the group the cue that the night is over. No source has the narrator nod to the police. Consistent with PR section 3.1.

**Doctor self-save.** Not addressed by any source opened in this run.

**First night.** The all4fun article has everyone open their eyes at the start of Day 1 together, so the first night is identification only. Consistent with PR.

**Bluffing as a rule.** The palermomafia.com FAQ says you can claim to be any character and whether others believe you is part of the game: «Το αν θα σε πιστέψουν είναι μέρος του παιχνιδιού!» Claims are not binding. This is new and matches the Palermo Game Team note in RC (R8).

**Beginners.** «αρχάριοι, ξεκινήστε με Κλασικό παιχνίδι» (beginners, start with the Classic game) in the palermomafia.com FAQ. Minimum players: at least 5 (same page: «τουλάχιστον 5 παίκτες»).

## 3. Narrator role and phrases

Phrases, Greek with English gloss, from the sources named:

- «Πέφτει η νύχτα στο Παλέρμο, κλείνουμε όλοι τα μάτια μας.» (Night falls on Palermo, we all close our eyes.) Qbrain, http://palermopaixnidi.blogspot.com/. Also pillowfights.gr: «Πέφτει η νύχτα στο Παλέρμο» (kidsproject variant too).
- «Θέλω τώρα οι δύο δολοφόνοι να ανοίξουν τα μάτια τους και να αναγνωριστούν μεταξύ τους...» (Now I want the two murderers to open their eyes and recognise each other.) Qbrain.
- «Ξημερώνει νέα μέρα στο Παλέρμο για όλους, εκτός από τον [όνομα].» (A new day dawns for everyone except [a named player].) Qbrain, used as a death announcement. The victim's name is part of the example and is not reproduced.
- «Μια νέα μέρα ξημερώνει στο Παλέρμο» (A new day dawns on Palermo.) pillowfights.gr and kidsproject.gr.
- «Οι δύο δολοφόνοι ανοίγουν τα μάτια τους και αλληλοκοιτάζονται.» (The two killers open their eyes and look at each other.) pillowfights.gr.
- «Τώρα, ανοίγει τα μάτια του ο αστυνομικός.» (Now the police officer opens his eyes.) pillowfights.gr.

Night order (Qbrain, http://palermopaixnidi.blogspot.com/): narrator says everyone closes eyes; the two killers open eyes and recognise each other; the informant opens eyes and the open killer raises a hand; the courtesan then identifies; at dawn the killers point to the victim for the narrator; the narrator announces the death.

**Humour.** The all4fun article describes the narrator telling the opening story «με τον δικό του χιουμοριστικό τρόπο» (in his own humorous way), and says laughter, shouts and quips accompanied the narrator's voice in the first night (https://www.all4fun.gr/portal/afierwmata/4411-peftei-i-nyxta-sto-palermo.html). The narrator is named in the article; this note does not repeat the name.

**Reveal ritual.** At the first day, everyone who took part «άνοιξαν τα μάτια τους» (opened their eyes) together (all4fun). An eliminated player «δείχνει την κάρτα του» (shows their card), which the article says gives the group information.

## 4. What Greek players describe as fun

- Shouting, arguments and staying up all night (pillowfights.gr: https://www.pillowfights.gr/oso-pataei-i-gata/i-nixta-peftei-sto-palermo/).
- Teasing over body language: «Ο Μάκης χαμογελάει πονηρά πρέπει να είναι ένοχος!» (Makis smiles slyly, he must be guilty!), in pillowfights.gr. A first name is in the article; this note keeps it generic.
- Accusing a silent friend: «Ο Γιώργος δεν λέει τίποτα, άρα κάτι κρύβει» (George says nothing, so he is hiding something), pillowfights.gr. Also in PR (S5).
- Bluffing, compared to poker: reading faces (pillowfights.gr).
- «η μόνη κερδισμένη είναι παρέα» (the only winner is the company), pillowfights.gr.
- Laughter, shouts and quips during the narration (all4fun).
- One review on the Artemis Codes app store page: «Νομίζω είναι από τα καλύτερα παιχνίδια» (I think it's one of the best games in this category). It praises quality, not fun specifically (https://apps.apple.com/gr/app/...id6759204428?l=el, read via fetch; the fetch summary is not a verbatim copy, so treat wording as unverified). The Palermo Mafia: Party page (https://apps.apple.com/app/id1558515349) shows 4.2 from 6 ratings and no review text, so it gives nothing on fun.

Not found: the «open your eyes» moment as a named ritual beyond the all4fun description; typical shouted lines beyond the accusation examples above.

## 5. Player counts and small tables

- Minimum 5 players (palermomafia.com FAQ; Palermo Mafia: Party listing: «5 or more players»; Artemis: 5–20 players, https://apps.apple.com/gr/app/...id6759204428).
- Beginners start with the Classic set (palermomafia.com FAQ).
- The Palermo Nights Classic set is 2 killers, 1 police, the rest citizens (apps.apple.com/app/id1558515349). Consistent with PR section 2.2.
- Random mode gives every player a unique random character after the 2 killers and police. This is a way to vary games without a host.
- Kidsproject frames the game as a family game for «sit together, play»; no count given.
- Nothing new on how groups of 5–7 play beyond the Classic set.

## Ideas that would transfer to a 6-seat AI table

1. A one-shot Dream card (Qbrain): one yes/no question to the narrator. Cheap, and gives a seat a private moment without a new phase.
2. A night-only Bulletproof and a Resurrection that triggers on any death (Qbrain). Both are one-shots and easy to narrate.
3. "Claiming any role is allowed and belief is part of the game" (palermomafia.com FAQ). An explicit prompt line to let AI seats bluff about their role.
4. A Random mode: draw a different unique role set each match (palermomafia.com FAQ, Palermo Mafia: Party listing). Gives the watcher variety.
5. Narrator tone: a short joking opener per game and a loud, quick "everyone opens eyes" moment at dawn of Day 1 (all4fun). Cheap to add to the narrator prompt.
6. A dead-player relay: the first ejected player states the night choice to the group (kidsproject, palermomafia.com). Matches the narrator-by-first-dead note in PR and RC; one voice that can carry the night without a human host.
